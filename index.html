Hamari story

17 January 2024The Day I First Saw You

Mere bhanje ka mundan tha, aur wahin main tumse pehli baar mila. Tum apni mummy ke paas khadi thi. Tumhari palkein jhuki hui thi, aur aankhon ke paas woh chhoti-chhoti veins dikh rahi thi.

Pata nahi kyun, but tumhe dekhte hi meri nazar tum par ruk gayi. Main bas tumhe dekhta reh gaya.

Mere paas sirf ek din tha, isliye himmat karke maine tumhe bulaya aur apne dil ki baat keh di. Maine bola ki agar aaj nahi bola, toh shayad phir kabhi mil nahi paunga.

Tum thodi ghabraayi, thodi interested hui... aur ek friendly si haan keh di. ❤️

Phir baatein badhi...
Muzaffarpur Railway StationJab train chalne lagi

Phir ek baar tumse Muzaffarpur Railway Station par mila. Main Delhi se aa raha tha, aur tum mujhse milne black suit aur black nail polish mein aayi thi. Tumhe dekhte hi main phir se bas dekhta reh gaya.

Mere paas zyada time nahi tha, kyunki meri train aage jaane wali thi. Tum mere paas baithi thi, nazrein jhuki hui, thodi sharmayi hui. Jitna time mila, humne usmein bahut saari baatein ki.

Lekin sabse emotional moment tab tha jab meri train chalne lagi. Tum station par khadi, palkein jhuka ke ro rahi thi, aur main bas tumhe dekhta hua door ja raha tha. ❤️

Phir tum important hoti gayi...
SitamarhiOur Beautiful Day

Teesri baar Sitamarhi mein mile, jab tumhari exam checking duty lagi thi. Maine tumhe pehli baar uniform mein dekha, aur tum usmein bhi bahut pyaari lag rahi thi.

Uske baad hum mall gaye, bahut masti ki, shopping ki aur saath mein bahut time spend kiya. Phir café gaye, khaya-piya aur khoob baatein ki. ❤️

Phir main Delhi chala gaya, aur jab wapas aaya...
Phir se SitamarhiJanki Mandir

Jab main Delhi se wapas aaya, toh hum phir Sitamarhi mein mile. Is baar Janki Mandir ke paas saath mein pooja ki, aur maine tumhe anklet pehnaya.

Tum us moment mein itni pyaari lag rahi thi ki bas dekhta rahun.

Lekin phir wahi mushkil moment, wapas jaana. Tumhare aansu aur tumhari tadhap dekhna mere liye sabse difficult tha. Long distance mein bhi humne bahut kuch saha. ❤️

Aur phir pata hi nahi chala kab tum meri favourite person ban gayi.
